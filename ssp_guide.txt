KARNATAKA SSP SCHOLARSHIP INFORMATION GUIDE

Portal: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in

Account creation: The referenced Karnataka SSP instructions describe account creation as a one-time process before applying when an account has not already been created

Aadhaar details: The referenced application procedure asks whether the student has Aadhaar and, for the stated process, records Aadhaar number, name as in Aadhaar, gender and CAPTCHA, followed by Aadhaar consent

Parent details: Parent details are entered during the account creation procedure described in the referenced instructions

Contact details: Student email ID and mobile number are provided, and an OTP is used in the referenced account creation procedure

Password requirement: The referenced procedure states a minimum of 8 characters with at least 1 digit for the account password

Student login: After account creation, the student logs in using the login ID, password and CAPTCHA

Scholarship application: The referenced procedure directs the student to select Apply for Post-Matric Scholarship from the student account homepage

10th details: The referenced procedure asks for the applicable 10th-standard board details and related registration/year information

Caste details: The referenced procedure includes religion, caste category, NSP Registration ID and relevant caste/income certificate RD numbers for the stated application process

Personal details: The referenced procedure includes home district, taluk, permanent address, PIN code, distance to college and Karnataka domicile information

College and course details: The referenced procedure includes college district/taluk, university, college, course, course start date, course year, discipline/combination, admission mode and admission year

Hosteller details: The referenced procedure asks whether the student is a hosteller

Previous year result: The referenced procedure asks for previous-year passed/result details according to the student's current course year

E-attestation: Karnataka instructions describe e-attestation of documents for applicable post-matric scholarship students and direct students to take original documents to the concerned e-attestation officer when required

Required documents: Required documents are scheme- and category-dependent; students should keep the documents specified by the applicable SSP scheme and instructions ready

Eligibility: SSP scholarship eligibility is scheme-specific and should be checked against the applicable department notification and current academic-year instructions

Deadline: Application deadlines are scheme- and academic-year-specific and are not fixed in this guide

Official support: For problems, the referenced Karnataka instructions advise students to contact the concerned institution/department and consult the applicable FAQ or official instructions; any helpline number should be verified against the current official notice

Process order: Account creation; Student login; Scholarship application

Scope: This guide is an educational summary based on cited Karnataka government instructions and is not an official eligibility decision or live SSP application-status service
